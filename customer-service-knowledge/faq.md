## Which product format should I choose?

Canvas is best for a permanent wall display or keepsake. A wall banner is lightweight and hangs with eyelets. A roll-up banner is freestanding and includes a stand.

Ask: Will it be displayed on a wall or freestanding?

## What does a roll-up banner include?

It includes custom design, an 85x200cm printed banner, stand, carry bag, pegs, box, up to five photos, and one free background removal. Extra photos and additional removals have separate fees.

## What does a custom themed wall banner include?

It includes custom design, eyelets, up to five photos, and one free main-photo background removal. It arrives ready to hang.

## Can you combine people or pets from different photos?

Yes. Ask the customer to send original photos, canvas/banner size, and total people/pets for a quote.

## Can you use blurry photos?

Ask for the original image and offer a quality assessment. Keep the customer-facing draft to those actions. Do not guarantee restoration, improvement quality, or print suitability before review.
