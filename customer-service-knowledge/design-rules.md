# Design Rules

## Before Design Starts

- Confirm the product and size.
- Confirm whether a reference should be copied closely or customized.
- Ask occasion, theme, colours, text, main photo, and orientation when relevant.
- Do not start custom design until the order is placed and half deposit is received.

## Photo Intake

- Ask for original files rather than screenshots, cropped copies, or edited copies.
- Multiple people/pets can be merged from different photos.
- Confirm total people/pets/faces because it affects price.
- Identify the main photo when background removal is included.
- For blurry/low-resolution photos, request the original image and offer an assessment.
- Do not guarantee restoration, improvement quality, or print suitability before the file is reviewed.
- Keep the customer-facing draft limited to requesting the original image and offering an assessment. Conclusions require file review.
- After assessment, request a better source image when needed.

## AI Design-Process Boundary

- AI may collect the product, size, original photos, wording, theme, and colour preferences.
- AI may say the team will review those details and guide the next step.
- Do not state arrangement, preview, revision, refund, or approval stages as policy unless a separate `CONFIRMED` rule permits it.
- Order-specific design or approval status always requires human review.

## Arrangement and Painting Stages

1. Show an arrangement/placement draft before painting.
2. Explain that the arrangement is not the final painting.
3. Confirm people, faces, photos, and placement.
4. Start painting only after arrangement confirmation.
5. Send the design/painting preview before printing.
6. Obtain explicit printing approval.

Replacing/removing photos after painting starts may require redrawing and an extra fee.

## Customization

- Text, halo, and angel wings are currently described as free.
- Object removal can be offered when technically feasible.
- Pose requests, such as making a parent hold a child, can be designed but may have an extra drawing charge.
- Background options may include sunrise, sunset, island scenery, sky/clouds, heavenly themes, Polynesian patterns, or mixed colours.
- If the customer has no preference, the designer may choose a suitable background after confirming any required text/colours.

## Natural-Looking Revisions

Use soft language:

- “We can adjust that.”
- “We can make it look more natural.”
- “We can move it slightly.”
- “We can make the photo blend better.”

Do not defend the draft or tell the customer they are wrong.

## Preview and Approval

- Send a preview before printing.
- Ask the customer to check all details carefully.
- Ask for all requested changes together.
- Up to two revisions are currently described as free.
- Once printing is explicitly confirmed, no further design changes can be made.
- The future system must store approval time, approved version, revision count, and print status in the order database.

## Memorial Orders

- Acknowledge emotional meaning without over-writing.
- Keep language gentle, personal, and respectful.
- Do not make emotional claims on the customer’s behalf.

## Outdoor Use

- Canvas is described as waterproof.
- Long-term outdoor exposure is not recommended.
- Historical customer use of more than three years outdoors is anecdotal, not a warranty.
