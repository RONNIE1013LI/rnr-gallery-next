# Revision and Refund Rules

Refund, cancellation, damaged goods, misprint/reprint, consumer rights, discount/compensation, and payment disputes are `UNRESOLVED — HIGH RISK`. Historical cases must not be converted into policy. These always require human escalation and current order evidence.

## Revision Policy Snapshot

- Customer sees a draft before printing.
- Up to two revisions are described as free.
- Ask the customer to list all changes together.
- Further changes may cost $30.
- Once printing is confirmed, no further design changes can be made.

The counting unit for revisions is not defined and must be confirmed before automation.

The allowance, current revision count, extra fee, approved version, and print status are `REALTIME_REQUIRED`.

## Photo Replacement / Redrawing

- Replacing or changing a photo after painting starts is described as $25 per person.
- Request the original, uncropped photo.
- Confirm the charge before redrawing.
- Product scope, partial changes, and exceptions are not defined.

## Refund Policy Snapshot

The current sources use three different trigger points:

1. No cancel/refund once design work has started.
2. Deposit becomes non-refundable once the draft is completed.
3. Once painted, the order is non-refundable.

These are not equivalent. Until the business confirms one authoritative policy, refund/cancellation questions must always be escalated.

## Complaint Handling SOP

1. Acknowledge the concern.
2. Give one short explanation.
3. Offer a practical solution.
4. State the next step.

Do not argue, blame the customer, or over-explain.

Before printing, offer a revision where appropriate. After printing, ask for a photo of the issue and escalate for assessment.

## Defect, Damage, or Wrong Item

No complete remedy policy is stored for:

- Transit damage.
- Printing defects.
- Wrong size/product.
- Colour differences.
- Customer-upload quality problems.
- Reprint, replacement, partial refund, or return shipping.

These cases require human review and must remain compatible with applicable consumer law.

## Safe Escalation Reply

> Sorry to hear that, and thank you for letting us know. Please send us a photo and your order reference so our team can check the best way to help.

Do not promise a refund, replacement, fee, or legal outcome before the order is reviewed.
