## Status Definitions

- `CONFIRMED`: Ronnie explicitly stated or corrected the rule. This does not make time-sensitive data permanent.
- `EVIDENCE-BASED`: Multiple historical interactions support the same practice, but Ronnie has not formally approved it as policy.
- `UNRESOLVED`: Evidence is insufficient, conflicting, ambiguous, or limited to code/templates or a single case.
- `REALTIME_REQUIRED`: The value or outcome can change and must be read from the current catalog, order, payment, production, pickup, or courier system.

When the original confirmation date was not preserved, the date field says so. The audit date is not substituted for a missing confirmation date.

## Automation Boundary

Only low-risk, static information with a current `CONFIRMED` rule may be used in an AI draft without live data. `EVIDENCE-BASED` content may guide style but must not be stated as policy. `UNRESOLVED`, `HIGH RISK`, and unavailable `REALTIME_REQUIRED` data must be blocked before model invocation. Phase 3.1 real-model validation does not expand this scope and never authorizes automatic sending.
