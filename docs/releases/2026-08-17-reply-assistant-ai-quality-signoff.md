- Human final version:

> A **canvas** is printed on artist canvas material and stretched over a wooden frame, so it has a solid, premium look and is ready to hang on the wall.
>
> A **wall banner** is printed on flexible banner material. It is lightweight and usually comes with eyelets around the edges so it can be hung or secured to a wall.
>
> So basically:
>
> - **Canvas** – more like permanent wall art, thicker and more premium.
> - **Wall banner** – lightweight, flexible and easier to transport, suitable for birthdays, events, memorials and temporary displays.
>
> Both can be fully customised with your photos, wording and design.
